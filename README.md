## What We Build

Tokio replaces static brand guidelines with an active, machine-readable knowledge graph.

A PDF is read by humans and ignored by AI. A traditional DAM stores assets without understanding them. Tokio reads guidelines, typography standards, visual rules, tone-of-voice docs, and motion principles—structuring every discipline into a queryable semantic model.

When two documents contradict each other, Tokio isolates the conflict side-by-side. You decide once. The canonical truth lives in the graph. A single source of truth.

Every asset generated by AI across your organization is validated against your brand graph before it ships.

**Your brand. Structured as code. Governed with mathematical certainty.**

---


## Why Tokio

**Active Knowledge, Not Static Decks.**  
Static guidelines rot inside shared drives. Tokio turns guidelines into an interconnected, machine-verifiable ontology. The difference between filing brand information and knowing it.

**Zero Silent Drift.**  
Nothing enters the knowledge graph without human sign-off. When documents disagree, Tokio surfaces both variants. You ratify the canonical rule once—eliminating hallucinated guidelines and unapproved variations.

**Output Validation at Scale.**  
Output nodes link your brand graph directly to content generation pipelines (decks, campaign collateral, social assets). Every output is audited against your brand truth before reaching production.

**Privacy by Constitution.**  
Engineered under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and EU GDPR adequacy standards. Your source documents, extracted nodes, and brand IP are never used to train public models.
